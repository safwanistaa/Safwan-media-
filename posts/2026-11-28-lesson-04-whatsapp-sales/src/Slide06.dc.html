STEP 3 · FOLLOW UP
Label every chat. Then follow up.
Labels sort chats by where the customer is. Each label has its own next message:
NEW CHAT
Reply within minutes, even at night (away message).
ASKED PRICE
Next day: “Still thinking? Your size is still in stock.”
ORDERED
Same day: confirm, delivery time, thank you.
DELIVERED
3 days later: “Happy with it? A photo would make our day.”
