AVOID THIS
4 mistakes that kill offers
Only cutting the priceFix: add value (a bonus, a promise) before you discount
Fake urgency: “ends today”, every dayFix: use real limits, and keep them
Five deals at onceFix: one clear offer per post
Talking about featuresFix: talk about the result the customer gets
