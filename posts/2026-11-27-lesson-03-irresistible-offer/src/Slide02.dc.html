WHAT IT IS
An offer is not your product. It's the whole deal.
A good offer answers 4 questions in the customer's head:
1
What do I get?The result, not just the item
بستفيد شنو؟
2
What's extra?A bonus that makes it feel generous
في زيادة؟
3
What if I don't like it?A promise that removes the risk
لو ما عجبني؟
4
Why now?A real reason not to wait
ليه هسي؟
