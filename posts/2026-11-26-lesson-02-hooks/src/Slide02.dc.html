WHAT IT IS
A hook = the first 1–3 seconds that make someone stop scrolling.
It's made of three things people get at the same moment:
1
What they seeThe first frame: a face, movement, something unexpected
البشوفو
2
What they hearYour first sentence
البسمعو
3
What they readThe short text on screen
البقروهو
If they don't stop, the rest of your video doesn't matter. Nobody sees it.
